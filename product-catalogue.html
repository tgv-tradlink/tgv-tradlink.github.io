Split Clamp — installation & ordering

How to install

5 steps
  1. 01
    Mark support pointsMark clamp positions along the pipe run as per the drawing or site practice.
  2. 02
    Fix the rodFix a bullet anchor in the slab and screw in the threaded rod (or bolt to a channel / wall).
  3. 03
    Mount the clampScrew the clamp boss onto the rod and set the height.
  4. 04
    Close around the pipeOpen the clamp, rest the pipe in the lower half, close the top half.
  5. 05
    Tighten evenlyTighten both screws in turns until the rubber grips — snug, not crushed. Lock the rod with a nut.

Buyer tips

  • Order by pipe size — a 2″ clamp fits 2″ NB pipe. For PPR / CPVC, check the outside diameter on the size reference page.
  • Rubber-lined clamps are the right choice on copper, stainless steel and plastic pipes.
  • Pair with BA series bullet anchors and threaded rod for ceiling hanging, or CH / CL series for channel mounting.
