About TGV TRADLINK

TGV TRADLINK is a manufacturer and wholesale distributor of industrial plumbing hardware, specialising in clamps, U-bolts, EPDM rubber products, fasteners, and related piping accessories. We design, produce, and supply to contractors, engineers, project managers, and industrial procurement teams across India.

As an in-house manufacturer, we maintain direct control over quality at every stage — from raw material selection to finished product — eliminating middlemen and passing the cost advantage directly to our buyers. Our manufacturing capabilities are backed by over a decade of industry expertise, ensuring consistent standards and competitive pricing across our entire product range.

Whether you need standard bulk orders or custom-specification hardware built to your exact drawings, TGV TRADLINK delivers with precision, accountability, and speed.
