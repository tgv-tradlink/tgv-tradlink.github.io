About TGV TRADLINK

TGV TRADLINK is an in-house manufacturer and wholesale distributor of industrial plumbing support hardware, specialising in clamps, U-bolts, EPDM rubber products, fasteners, and related accessories. We design, produce, and supply directly to contractors, engineers, and industrial procurement teams across India.

By maintaining direct control over quality at every stage — from raw material selection to finished product — we eliminate middlemen and pass the cost advantage directly to our buyers. Whether you need standard bulk orders or custom-specification hardware built to your drawings, TGV TRADLINK delivers with precision, accountability, and speed.
