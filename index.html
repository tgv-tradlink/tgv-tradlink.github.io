01 — Who we are

Your wholesale partner for pipe support hardware.

TGV TRADLINK supplies industrial hardware and piping support solutions to contractors, engineers and infrastructure projects across India.

Our range covers split clamps, U-bolt clamps, leaf clamps, sprinkler hanging clamps and G.I. & S.S. clamps built for strength and precision — alongside EPDM rubber products, cantilever systems with base plates, anchor fasteners and ISI-marked butterfly valves. Together they cover plumbing, fire-fighting and industrial piping work end to end.

Strong manufacturer partnerships let us offer bulk availability, cost-effective pricing and timely dispatch — so your project runs on schedule and within budget.
