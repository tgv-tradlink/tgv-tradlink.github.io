Who We Are

Your Trusted Industrial
Wholesale Partner

TGV TRADLINK is a trusted wholesale distributor of industrial hardware and piping support solutions, catering to contractors, engineers, and infrastructure projects across India. Our product portfolio covers a wide spectrum including split clamps, U-bolt clamps, leaf clamps, sprinkler hanging clamps, and G.I. & S.S. clamps designed for strength, durability, and precision.

We also specialize in high-performance EPDM rubber products, cantilever systems with base plates, anchor fasteners, and ISI-marked butterfly valves — ensuring complete support for plumbing, fire-fighting, and industrial piping applications. Every product is sourced and supplied with a focus on reliability, consistency, and long-term performance in demanding environments.

With strong manufacturer partnerships and a commitment to quality, we deliver cost-effective solutions, bulk availability, and timely dispatch — helping our clients execute projects efficiently, on schedule, and within budget.
